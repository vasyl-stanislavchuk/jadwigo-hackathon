# dl-1u6fn: wave 1 plan, the remote MCP for parents

Epic `dl-1u6fn`. Wave label `wave:dl-1u6fn-1`. A parent connects Jadwigo from claude.ai, ChatGPT,
Claude Code or Muse at `https://app.jadwigo.pl/api/mcp`, signs in with OAuth, reads everything they
can read in the app (and nothing from another pod), searches the bank, and drafts, checks and assigns
a lesson. The epic's design field holds the whole plan.

Wave 1 builds the pieces nothing blocks: the OAuth spike, the real bank in the engine, the publish
route, the play page for a named child, and the endpoint with one tool.

## Decisions in force

All of them are the owner's, from 2026-11-02, in `jadwigo-hackathon/decisions/`:

- `2026-11-02-a-parent-s-own-ai-app-connects-jadwigo-s-remote-mcp-by-oauth-2-1`: OAuth 2.1, with
  Supabase Auth as the authorization server (this closed `dl-5ssh.21`).
- `2026-11-02-through-the-remote-mcp-the-parent-s-own-ai-writes-the-lesson-and`: the parent's AI
  writes the lesson, and our verbs check it on the server.
- `2026-11-02-a-parent-s-yes-in-their-own-ai-chat-is-the-confirmation-lesson-p`: a yes in chat
  publishes and assigns (this closed `dl-hin8.3`).
- `2026-11-02-the-report-a-parent-s-ai-reads-through-the-mcp-is-the-newest-sto`: the report is the
  newest stored row.
- `2026-11-02-a-child-s-data-names-included-may-go-to-the-ai-app-the-parent-co`: the family's data
  going to the parent's own app is the parent's export.
- `2026-11-02-the-remote-mcp-reads-everything-a-parent-can-read-in-the-app-and`: every parent read
  is available, from the parent's own pod plus the bank.
- `2026-11-02-a-lesson-confirmed-through-the-mcp-reaches-the-child-as-a-play-l`:
  `/play/<asg>?learner=<lrn_>`, with no migration.
- `2026-11-02-a-lesson-holds-at-most-20-questions-however-it-was-made`.
- `2026-11-02-the-remote-mcp-s-lesson-tools-run-inside-the-api-lambda-s-10-s-l`: the 10 s risk is
  accepted.
