# dl-z5ja: wave 2 plan, hardening the engine and the chat pane before the adapters

Epic `dl-z5ja`. Wave label `wave:dl-z5ja-2`. Wave 1 (`2026-10-31-wave-1.md`) proved the chat →
lesson loop locally and landed on main (#405 to #410). Wave 2 hardens it while it is still
engine-neutral. It fixes what the wave-1 reviews found, adds the safety checks the research asked
for, and draws the run contract v2 (`dl-z5ja.50`, #413) in the chat pane.

Wave 2 still adds no migration, no network call, no route and no paid call. The adapters (Postgres,
pgmq, Realtime, `HttpMuse`, the step host) are now **wave 3**, because they wait on the owner (see
"What waits").

- `docs/worklog/dl-z5ja/2026-10-31-wave-1.md`: its Lead decisions 1 to 19 still hold, and this plan
  adds to them.
- `docs/research/posthog-ai-ux-patterns.md`: sections 5, 6, 10, 15.3 and 16.

## Lead decisions

20. **Wave 2 builds on run contract v2.** `dl-z5ja.50` (#413) lands first. It adds:
    - `tool_result.view`;
    - `message.citations`;
    - the `approval_request.choose` form;
    - `approval_resolved.feedback` and `answers`;
    - the `plan` event;
    - `model_response.model` and `tier`;
    - `ToolState` `rejected`.

    Lanes draw and use these fields. They never change them.
21. **A steer is logged** (step 0, `dl-z5ja.51`). A new `steer` run event
    `{text, source: "chat" | "lesson_edit", question_ids?}` joins the union, and the inbox's `steer`
    payload gains `source?` and `question_ids?`.
    - The runner appends the event, fenced on `attempt`, when it takes a steer off the inbox.
    - It builds the next call's instructions from the turn's logged steers, not from what it took,
      so a redelivered step keeps them (`dl-z5ja.42`).
    - The fold draws a steer as a parent chat item marked as a steer.
22. **A steer that arrives during the final call keeps the turn open** for one more step. It is
    never dropped (`dl-z5ja.42`).
23. **The answer check is pure and lives in `packages/core/src/run`** (`dl-z5ja.26`). It takes a
    message's text and the turn's events, and returns the numbers and status words no
    `tool_result` gave.
    - The core lane writes it.
    - The runner lane calls it before it writes an agent `message`. On a miss it asks Muse to
      correct once, then writes an `error` event.
24. **The untrusted-content fence is a module, `packages/jadwigo-ai/src/fence/`** (`dl-z5ja.47`).
    - It is a tagged block that says the text is data and never instructions.
    - Markup is neutralised (`<` `>` become `‹` `›`, `](` becomes `]‹`).
    - The runner wraps every piece of text a child or a photo wrote before it enters
      `MuseRequest.input`.
25. **AI-written lesson text passes the hand-written lesson's checks** (`dl-z5ja.31`), inside the
    verbs: `lesson_check` and `packages/copy`'s harsh-word check run on every `lesson_revision`. A
    revision with an error finding can't be published.
26. **The web lane reads only contracts and the fold's `RunView`.** A gap in either is a message to
    the lead.

  merged it, and that is the last item in the runner's queue.

  rebase.
- The lead keeps each PR's hand-backs after its lane ends.

## Briefs

Wave 1's rules apply:
- a lane's brief is its issue;
- each lane gets its whole queue at once;
- one PR per issue, titled `<id>: <what>`;
- `gh pr ready` and `gh pr merge --auto --squash` once step 0 is on main;
- notes on the issue say what ran and what didn't;
- a lane ends with `DONE`, `DONE_WITH_CONCERNS`, `NEEDS_DECISIONS` or `BLOCKED`.

Done when:
- all 13 lane issues and step 0 are merged;
- `make check` is green on main;
- the 9 wave-1 scenarios and the 2 steer scenarios pass with `EXPECTED_RED` empty;
- `apps/web/e2e` drives one turn at a phone viewport (CI).
