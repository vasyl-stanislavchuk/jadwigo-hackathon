# dl-z5ja: wave 6 plan, PDF, DOCX and HEIC

Epic `dl-z5ja`, issue epic `dl-z5ja.11`. Wave label `wave:dl-z5ja-6`. Wave 6 lets a parent attach a
worksheet as a PDF or a DOCX, or an iPhone photo as a HEIC the browser cannot decode. One server-side
converter turns each into page JPEGs when a turn reads it, and the model sees those pages exactly as
it sees photos. Wave 5 (`2026-11-01-wave-5.md`) built the JPEG path this rides on.

## Lead decisions

58. **One converter Lambda, `apps/converter`** (`dl-z5ja.11.8`): Python 3.12 in a container, a uv
    project.
    - LibreOffice turns DOCX into PDF, `pdftoppm` turns PDF pages into JPEGs, and `heif-convert`
      turns HEIC into JPEG.
    - Pillow re-encodes every page to a JPEG of at most 2000 px and 4 MB, with no metadata.
    - Pages go to `pods/<pod>/attachments/<id>/p<n>.jpg`, with a `pages.json` manifest. A second
      call returns the manifest as it is.
    - It takes at most `LIMITS.documentPages` (10) pages and refuses an encrypted PDF.
    - The runner invokes it over IAM, with S3 keys, never bytes.
59. **The runner converts on read** (`dl-z5ja.11.7`): the API's 10 s limit is too short for
    LibreOffice.
    - A non-JPEG `MessageAttachment` goes through a `Converter` port, and each page becomes an
      `input_image` behind the photo fence.
    - A turn sends at most `LIMITS.imagesPerTurn` (20) images.
    - A failed conversion is a plain error line in the chat, never a silent drop.
    - The file's name never reaches the model.
60. **The API takes 4 kinds** (`dl-z5ja.11.10`): `image/jpeg`, `image/heic`, `application/pdf`
    and DOCX.
    - A non-JPEG is checked by its first bytes at send and is at most `LIMITS.documentBytes`
      (10 MB).
    - It is copied to `pods/<pod>/attachments/<id>.<ext>`.
    - Step 0 (`dl-z5ja.11.11`) widens the contract and the table:
      - `media_type` becomes a text column with a check, because a DOCX's media type is longer than
        a Postgres enum label;
      - `width` and `height` are nullable, and only a JPEG needs them;
      - `name` is kept for the chip.

      Until `dl-z5ja.11.10` lands, `createAttachment` refuses a non-JPEG with 409
      `AttachmentMismatch`.
61. **Previews** (`dl-z5ja.11.10`, `dl-z5ja.11.12`). `AttachmentRead.preview_url` is the
    converter's `p1.jpg` once a turn has read the file, and null before. The composer and the
    thread show a document chip (an icon, the name, the kind) until a preview exists. The page never
    re-encodes a document or a HEIC.
62. **The lanes build on fakes.** The owner builds and pushes the converter image and applies the
    infra, as `lambda-docx-conversion`'s `make image` and `make deploy` do.

## Briefs

The wave 1 to 5 rules apply: one PR per issue, titled `<id>: <what>`, targeting `main`; `gh pr ready`
and `gh pr merge --auto --squash` once ready; a stack updates by merge; notes on the issue say what ran
and what did not; a lane ends with `DONE`, `DONE_WITH_CONCERNS`, `NEEDS_DECISIONS` or `BLOCKED`; paid,
careful and outward steps are the owner's.

Done when:
- the converter's pytest turns synthetic DOCX, PDF and HEIC files into metadata-free JPEGs of at
  most 2000 px, refuses an 11-page and an encrypted PDF, and returns a stored manifest unchanged;
- the api int suite attaches a PDF, a DOCX and a HEIC by their first bytes, refuses a renamed file,
  and answers `preview_url` once `p1.jpg` exists;
- a runner scenario shows a 3-page PDF and a DOCX as page image parts behind the fence, 25 pages
  capped at 20 with a note, and a failed conversion as an error line;
- the web tests show a document chip with no re-encode, and a HEIC uploaded as it is when the
  decoder fails;
- `make check-all` is green on `main`.
