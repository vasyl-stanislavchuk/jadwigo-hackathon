# dl-n9fr: wave 4 plan

Epic `dl-n9fr`, the question bank: from the experiment into the monorepo and the production
platform. Lead: the session that wrote this, 2026-10-28. Wave label: `wave:dl-n9fr-4`.

## Goal

The bank that is live from `jadwigo-bank` becomes reachable from the monorepo through one
authorized route, the `jw` CLI and MCP tools move here, the 2 Terraform roots shake hands, the
Khan pipeline homes in the bank repo, and the service's known gaps close. The design is
`docs/research/bank-into-the-monorepo.md` (`dl-n9fr.32`). The bank itself never enters this repo.
