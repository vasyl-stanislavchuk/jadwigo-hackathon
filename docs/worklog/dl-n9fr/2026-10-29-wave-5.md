- No lane applies, migrates, uploads, creates a remote, pushes, merges or runs `gh pr ready`; those are
  the lead's under the grant.
- No Khan item text or picture in a test, a fixture or a handoff; counts only.
- No secret value anywhere: name the files and the keys, never a value.
- A lane that needs a path it does not own stops and names it in the handoff.
