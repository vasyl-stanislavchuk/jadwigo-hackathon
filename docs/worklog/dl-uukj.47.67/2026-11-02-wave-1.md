# dl-uukj.47.67: waves 1 and 2, the adult layer for the parent app and jadwigo.pl

Epic `dl-uukj.47.67`. Waves `wave:dl-uukj.47.67-1` (step 0, kit, site) and `wave:dl-uukj.47.67-2` (the
parent screens). The app now has two audiences on purpose. Adults (the parent app, sign-in, consent,
jadwigo.pl's frame) get Geist, radius 6/8/10, 1 px lines, flat controls and an ink-blue primary. Children
(the lesson player, review tiles, any lesson preview, the lesson screens the site shows) keep Zeszyt as
it is.

S1–S23). Where the handoff and this plan disagree, the handoff wins unless a lead decision below says why.

## Lead decisions (taken overnight 2026-11-02 for the owner to review; each is reversible)

1. **The boundary is one attribute, `data-audience`.** `<html data-audience="adult">` on `/parent…` and
   `/oauth…` (set by the app by route) and on every jadwigo.pl page (Base.astro). `data-audience="kid"` on
   `LessonTheme`'s root and on the site's child sections. Unset (`:root`) stays Zeszyt, so a kid route
   needs no change. Portals inherit it from `<html>`; a lesson-kit popover opened inside a lesson preview
   is marked kid by its register.
2. **Adult overrides live in one block in `foundation.css`**, `[data-audience="adult"]`, that maps both
   the shadcn variables (§2) and the `--jw-*` roles today's parent screens read. That restyles every
   existing parent screen at once; the lanes then replace hand-rolled parts. A kid restore block,
   `[data-audience="adult"] :is([data-audience="kid"], [data-lesson-theme])`, puts every role, size and
   the font back. New hexes are tier-1 primitives only.
3. **One Button.** `packages/ui` Button gains the §6.1 variants (`default` = primary, `secondary`,
   `outline`, `ghost`, `quiet`, `link`) and sizes (`sm` 28, `control` 34, `lg` 40, `icon`). It is flat in
   adult (press and edge are 0 there) and keeps the pressed edge in kid. `chat/button.tsx` is deleted by
   the chat lane (`.10`).
4. **Child tones before the contract has them:** a child's tone is its index in the pod's learners
   sorted by `created_at`, mod 10, computed in the web app. Stable while no child is deleted. The stored
   tone (`dl-5ssh.145`, a migration and a contract change, one-way doors the owner merges) replaces it.
5. **Dependencies, kept to what the handoff names:** `@fontsource/geist` and `@fontsource/geist-mono`
   5.3.0 (OFL, self-hosted like Andika), `@tanstack/react-table` 9.3.1 (D11), `sonner` 2.0.8 (D17).
   Not added: `cmdk` (no search, D13; the Combobox is a radio list in a Popover), `vaul` (the phone
   Drawer is the existing Sheet from the bottom), a date picker (the Data facet has presets; "Własny
   zakres" is parked), an OTP library (one numeric input drawn as 6 cells).
6. **Archive acts at once with Cofnij** (S4, P4). If the owner keeps the confirm dialog (§13.1), only the
   toast call changes. A lane builds archive only where an archive route exists today.
7. **jadwigo.pl: which line is which audience** (`dl-w0b4.122`). Adult: the header, nav, burger, footer,
   language switch, cookie banner, every button and link, section headings and parent copy, StepPath (it
   explains the loop to the parent), PartnerBlock (it shows the parent app's child page), the promises
   band, EndBand, /jak-to-dziala and /jadwigo-ai bands, prose pages, sign-in. Kid: HeroQuestion's sheet
   (the playable question and its tiles), the Gallery's lesson cards, /jak-to-dziala step 2's question
   card, Backdrop's sums and stars. Illustrations are the illustrated world and keep their own art. The
   page's structure, direction (1a) and words do not change in this wave: picking 1a/1b/1c
   (`dl-w0b4.100`) and copy stay the owner's.
8. **Copy:** each lane adds its own keys in `packages/copy` (types, pl, en, review sidecars, `bun run
   messages`). A conflict with another lane's keys is resolved by merging `main`, never by rebase.
9. **The e2e font checks change from Andika to Geist** on sign-in (`parent.spec.ts`,
   `social-sign-in.spec.ts`) in step 0; every kid assertion stays.
10. **docs.jadwigo.pl keeps Andika** for now (`dl-rr71`); its tokens page documents both audiences.
