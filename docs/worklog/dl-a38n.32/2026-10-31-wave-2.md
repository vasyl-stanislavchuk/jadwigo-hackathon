## The flow, end to end

`make ready` (conv-pl 4) runs locally: convert `en` and `pl`, stamp both with the runner, derive,
`build_sets`; it prints one readiness report per language (items, ready, waiting by reason, refused by
rule and by converter reason, re-admitted since the previous file, sets indexed and skipped) and writes
it to `<KHAN_DIR>/readiness.json`. `jw question readiness` (loop 1) reads it, and so does the drop's
Readiness section (loop 2). Every cause carries the bead that fixes it, assigned with
`jw question refusals assign`; the next run reports what the fix re-admitted. The owner's steps stay
the paid or outward ones: the vision pass, `sync up`, the index, the gate, the publish.

## The order

1. Lanes start together; conv-pl and conv-en share `to_family.py` by region (the stamp hook is
   conv-pl's, the rest conv-en's) and both work in their own worktree off the bank's `main`.
2. conv-pl 1 lands first among the bank PRs; the others merge up by `git merge`.
3. Each bank PR is reviewed by the lead and merged into the bank's `main` through `gh pr merge`.
4. After conv-pl 2: the owner's vision pass, then `make ready`, `sync up`.
5. After every lane: `make ready` once more, the counts on `dl-a38n.32`, the drop refreshed, then the
   owner's `dl-a38n.32.15`.

Stacks update by `git merge`, never by rebase. The builder never reviews its own work. The lead takes
the steward's hand-backs on the monorepo PRs and merges the bank PRs.
