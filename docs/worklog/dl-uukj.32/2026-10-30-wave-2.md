## What step 0 gives the lanes

- **`apps/web/src/nav/`**: `location.ts` (`useLocation`, `navigate`, `queryLanguage`, `LANGUAGES`)
  and `NavLink.tsx`. Every product screen imports from there; the playground re-exports them
  (`playground/route.ts`, `playground/shell/NavLink.tsx`) and `PlaygroundScreen` loads lazily.
- **`apps/web/src/review/outcome.ts`**: `ENDED_WORD`, `ENDED_ICON`, `ENDED_TONE`, `TONE_CLASS`,
  `Tone`. The parent's `AttemptReview` tiles and the overview's `OUTCOME_TILE` already read it.
- **`ReviewStep`** takes `category?: Category` (default `"child"`) and passes it to every line and
  to the family view.
- **Copy keys** (`packages/copy`): `close`, `parent_review_try_line` ("{time} · {help}"),
  `parent_overview_sheet_line` ("{tries} · {help} · {time}"), `parent_overview_ratio` ("{n}/{m}"),
  `parent_attempt_line` ("{say}: {ended}"), `parent_load_failed`; and the adult lines for
  `play_ended_*`, `review_no_answer`, `review_praise`. All `reviewed: false` (`dl-uukj.32.6`).
- **The kit** (`@jadwigo/ui`, once #204 and #208 are on main): `Page` (`width="prose" | "kid"`),
  `Heading`, `Label`, `Field`, `Link`, `Notice`, `OutcomeTile`, `Input kind="text"`,
  `Card size="compact"`, `Button size="control"`, `RadioGroup`, `SegmentedControl`, `Tabs`,
  `SideMenu`; the action Button (#205) after #200.
- **The check**: `apps/web/src/tokens.test.ts` with one allowlist per lane directory. A lane
  deletes its `tokens.allow.json` when it is empty.

## Shared paths

- `packages/copy`, `packages/ui` and `packages/lesson-kit` change only through the lead or a
  wave-1 lane. A missing key, part or token is a message to the lead.
- `apps/web/src/review/outcome.ts` is read by `web-child` and `web-parent`; its exports change only
  through the lead.
- `apps/web/e2e/*` is split by file as the Owns column says; `smoke.spec.ts` and the auth helpers
  are the lead's.
- Lane PRs target `main` (step 0 of this wave is small and merges first); they stay drafts until
  this PR is on main. A stack updates by `git merge`, never by rebase.
- The lead reads `md merge-steward status` for every PR of the epic and takes the hand-backs
  once the lanes stop.

## Stop rules for a lane

- A screen needs a part, a token or a copy key the kit does not have.
- A `data-*` hook an e2e spec queries would have to go.
- A test you did not write and did not touch fails, in your package or another.
- The same step failed 3 times with no new information.
