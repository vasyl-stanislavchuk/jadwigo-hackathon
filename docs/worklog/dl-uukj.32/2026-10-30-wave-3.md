left four gaps for the owner (`dl-uukj.32.7`). The owner answered them in Claude Design on
2026-10-30. This wave builds the answers: parent pages read an adult type scale and keep a visible
focus, the parent's outcomes gain a soft green and a dashed tile, a wrong try then "Jeszcze nie

and 36 on a phone, and the System page shows all of it.

## Shared paths

- `packages/ui/src/styles/**` and `packages/copy` change only through the lead. A missing token,
  utility, tone or copy key is a message to the lead.
- `apps/web/src/review/outcome.ts` is the lead's; `web-parent` owns `ReviewStep.tsx` for its
  `badge` prop only, and the child's default stays as it is.
- Lane PRs target `main` and stay drafts until step 0 is on main. A stack updates by `git merge`,
  never by rebase.
- The lead reads `md merge-steward status --epic dl-uukj.32` and takes every hand-back once the
  lanes stop.

## Stop rules for a lane

- A part, token, tone or copy key the lane needs is not on main.
- A `data-*` hook an e2e spec queries would have to go.
- A test you did not write and did not touch fails, in your package or another.
- The same step failed 3 times with no new information.
