# Who is Jadwigo for first, what do we tell them, and how do the first families find it?

Issue: `dl-w0b4.16`

## The answer in 5 lines

- **First audience:** the parents of 1 class, the 18 classmates in the pilot (`dl-e5c4`), with the class teacher as the person they trust. Synthesis started inside 1 school of about 8 children and Prodigy inside 1 classroom, so a closed group is a proven start. It gives depth, not reach.
- **What we tell them:** "free, no ads" is now the minimum every competitor promises. What sets Jadwigo apart is what Polish parents already worry about: guilt about screens, doubt that "educational" apps teach, and ads. Our answers are no pressure, credited sources and data that belongs to the parent. Section 4 gives 3 ways to lead, in Polish, for the parent and for the child.
- **The path:** first the hackathon demo on 4 November, then the pilot class through its teacher and its parents' group. After that comes word of mouth from those parents, and from teachers who already enter pupils in Alfik Matematyczny.
- **Limits that shape it:** no message may urge a child to buy or to ask for anything (UOKiK). Analytics stay cookieless. Email is collected only from a parent with a separate opt-in, and nothing about a child is sent anywhere.
- **What only the owner can choose** is listed in the outcome block at the end. There are 4 choices: the first audience, the lead message, how the pilot invite reaches parents, and whether the landing page collects emails.

## Method

- **Read:** the jadwigo-marketing charter and its decisions in force. Also the hackathon's rules, as recorded in `david-learns/notes/meta-global-ai-developer-hackathon-dates-models-tracks-and-selec`. Also 3 research notes in david-learns, used read-only: `parent-preferences.md`, `engagement-sentiment.md` and `engagement-by-age.md`.
- **Web research on 2026-10-27:**
  - how 8 comparable products reached their first users
  - Polish surveys on parents and screens
  - where Polish parents gather
  - the rules on marketing to families
  - how a tool enters a grade 1 to 3 classroom
- **Not done:** no interviews with parents or teachers, and no message tested on anyone. Most first-user stories come from vendors and the trade press, and none gives counts for the first 100 to 1,000 families. I could find no account of how any Polish product got its first users.

## 1. Comparable products

| Product | First audience | Channel | Lead message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prodigy | 1 classroom, then teachers | email to teachers; a grade 2 teacher signed up all 54 pupils | free, fits the Ontario curriculum |
| Khan Academy Kids | parents of ages 2 to 5 | app store, press, an existing brand | "100% free, no ads, no subscriptions" |
| Duolingo ABC | parents of ages 3 to 6 | app store, press, an existing brand | free, no ads, works offline |
| DragonBox | parents with iPads | word of mouth, press; kept away from schools on purpose | a game, not "algebra" |
| Synthesis | about 8 children of 1 school's community | a closed community, then about 1,000 subscribers within months | "Elon Musk's school" |
| Matific | ministries and schools | pilots with ministries (Croatia, Ukraine) | efficacy studies |
| Squla (Poland) | parents, preschool to grade 6 | paid access, teacher-written quizzes | fits the podstawa programowa |
| WSiP "Litery i cyfry" | parents | a known textbook brand; #1 in the Polish App Store's education category on its first day | the publisher's name |

Sources: [Prodigy](https://altitudeaccelerator.ca/a-free-math-game-kids-want-to-play-prodigy/) and [its FTC complaint](https://www.axios.com/2021/02/19/prodigy-math-game-ftc-complaint), [Khan Academy Kids](https://thejournal.com/Articles/2018/07/12/Khan-Academy-Kids-Launches-for-Early-Learners.aspx), [Duolingo ABC](https://techcrunch.com/2020/03/26/duolingos-new-app-teaches-children-how-to-read-and-write/), [DragonBox](https://qz.com/390854/the-video-game-that-teaches-algebra-to-4-year-olds), [Synthesis](https://thehustle.co/meet-synthesis-the-edtech-startup-scaling-elon-musks-ad-astra-school), [Matific](https://www.matific.com/home/why-matific/validation), [Squla](https://www.polishscience.pl/en/?p=2794), [WSiP](https://imagazine.pl/?p=40137).

**What carries over.** Every product that grew fast had either an existing brand or a closed first community. Jadwigo has no brand yet, but it does have a community: 1 class.

Prodigy is also a warning. It grew through teachers, then sold upgrades to the children it reached, and 22 groups filed an FTC complaint over it. Jadwigo's parent account and no-pressure rule close that door by design. The message must say so.

## 2. What parents worry about

- **Guilt, and a shift to quality.** 71.6% of Polish parents sometimes feel worry or guilt about their child's device use, and 92% see benefits for development. Parents now judge what a child watches more than how long. This comes from a vendor-reported survey with no published method ([Super Biznes, 2026](https://superbiz.se.pl/wiadomosci/koniec-wojny-z-tabletami-polscy-rodzice-patrza-dzis-na-cos-zupelnie-innego-aa-CXmh-5mBZ-9saP.html)).
- **Screens are everywhere already.** 84% of children aged 5 to 7 and 96% of those aged 8 to 11 use devices. They spend about 60 minutes a day at 5 to 7, and about 2 hours at 8 to 11 ([CBOS via chip.pl, 2025](https://www.chip.pl/2025/08/cbos-niepokojacy-trend-ekrany-pochlaniaja-nasze-dzieci)). So the question is no longer whether a child uses a screen. It is what the screen does for them.
- **Doubt about "educational" apps.** Polish researchers on the "Brzdąc w sieci" study found most popular "educational" apps have low educational value, while parents believe they help ([Bankier](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Polskie-przedszkolaki-przyklejone-do-ekranow-Zalecenia-WHO-na-nic-9012598.html)). Showing where the questions come from (Alfik, Khan Academy) answers that doubt with something a parent can check.
- **School matters.** 75% of Poles say a good primary school has the biggest influence on a child's future career ([CBOS via Wprost](https://www.wprost.pl/48377)).
- **Ads and privacy.** 95% of apps for children under 5 carried advertising in a Michigan study, and parents rarely noticed ([The Swaddle](https://www.theswaddle.com/95-of-kids-apps-contain-at-least-one-type-of-advertising-says-study)). Parents of 6 to 10-year-olds hold strong privacy expectations and say they get little help choosing apps ([arXiv 1809.10841](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.10841)).
- **Not found:** no Polish survey tests "school readiness", "catching up" or "confidence" as messages.

Our own research adds 1 finding. Children do better when parents set a few limits ahead of time and leave the small choices to the child. They do worse when parents control the work. See `parent-preferences.md` §4 in david-learns, citing Vasquez and others 2016 and Patall, Cooper and Robinson 2008. That is the parent-steers, child-chooses split the charter already holds, and the message can lean on it.

## 3. Channels and rules

- **The e-register is the official channel.** About 17,000 schools use Librus or Vulcan ([Telepolis](https://www.telepolis.pl/wiadomosci/cyfrowa-szkola-e-dziennili-librus-koszty-pod-lupa-poslow)).
- **The working channel is the class chat.** The class teacher may agree a WhatsApp or Messenger group with parents ([one Poznań school's rules](https://www.pss.poznan.pl/sites/default/files/2026-04/Standardy_komunikacji_w_PSS_tekst.pdf)). Messenger reaches 48.2% of Poland ([NapoleonCat, 2024](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/messenger-users-in-poland)).
- **No Polish study measures how tools spread through class groups.** Parents do rank trusted people, other parents and experts among their key sources ([money.pl, WP panel, not representative](https://www.money.pl/gospodarka/09-2023-rodzic-jako-konsument-6983562040207872a.html)).
- **Teachers:**
  - From 1 September 2026, grade 1 follows a new curriculum, "Reforma26. Kompas Jutra", and schools are re-checking their programmes now ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/rok-szkolny-20252026-dla-czesci-uczniow-ostatni-rok-z-dotychczasowa-podstawa-programowa)).
  - A teacher may recommend a free home tool. Bringing an organisation into the school needs the head teacher and the parents' council ([Prawo oświatowe, art. 86](https://lexlege.pl/prawo-oswiatowe/art-86/)).
  - Alfik Matematyczny reaches teachers in over 1,500 schools, and 7,448 grade 3 pupils took part in 2024 ([SP28 Kielce](https://sp28.kielce.eu/sites/default/files/doc/2024/01/05/nid2819/ogolnopolski-konkurs-alfik-matematyczny-2023.pdf)).
- **The rules:**
  - A message may not urge a child to buy, or to get an adult to buy. UOKiK has charged influencers under this rule ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/uokik-postawil-zarzuty-influencerom-za-reklamy-kierowane-do-dzieci)).
  - In Poland a child under 16 needs a parent's consent for an online service ([GDPR Art. 8](https://gdpr-text.com/read/article-8/?lang1=en)). A parent account, with a child who never logs in, keeps the parent as the user.
  - Analytics that store nothing on the device need no consent banner under Art. 399 of the electronic communications law ([standardyprawa.pl](https://standardyprawa.pl/akt/532/art/63604)). That is our reading of it, since we found no regulator guidance. It matches the cookieless PostHog plan (`dl-w0b4.14`).
  - Marketing email to a parent needs its own opt-in.

## 4. Lead messages in Polish

Each option has 1 line for the parent, who decides, and 1 for the child, who plays. That follows `jadwigo-marketing/decisions/2026-10-27-parents-decide-and-children-study-both-feel-at-home-on-the-landi`. Every line is a draft for a native speaker to review. None uses points, clocks or a bare "źle", and none asks a child to get anything from an adult.

| | For the parent | For the child | Answers which worry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Clarity** | Zobacz, z czym Twoje dziecko radzi sobie dobrze, a co warto jeszcze poćwiczyć. | Zagraj w krótką lekcję. Możesz się pomylić. To część nauki. | doubt that apps teach; school |
| **B. Calm** | Nauka bez punktów, zegarów i rankingów. Dziecko ćwiczy w swoim tempie, a Ty decydujesz, co dalej. | Tu nikt cię nie pogania. | guilt about screens |
| **C. Trust** | Zadania z Alfika Matematycznego i Khan Academy, zawsze z podanym źródłem. Dane Twojego dziecka należą do Ciebie. | Prawdziwe zagadki, krok po kroku. | doubt about "educational" claims; privacy |

2 lines belong under any lead: "bezpłatnie" (free) and "bez reklam" (no ads). Every competitor says them, so leaving them out would read as a gap. The featured lesson is the proof. Option A promises what it already shows, a picture of where a child stands, and the word "diagnostyczna" (diagnostic) never appears (`david-learns/decisions/2026-10-23-diagnostic-lessons-are-the-featured-way-in-and-a-diagnostic-buil`).

## 5. The path to the first families

1. **4 November, the hackathon.** The judges are the audience. The theme "Build for Your World" fits a parent building for their own child's class. The demo is the live jadwigo.pl, a featured lesson, and the parent screen.
2. **The pilot class (`dl-e5c4`).** 18 families, reached through the class teacher and the class's parents' chat. A short Polish letter says what Jadwigo is, what it stores and what it never does. Every child plays the same first lesson, and a parent registers to get the second.
3. **Word of mouth from those parents.** A parent who registers is the one who shares the link. Nothing in the product asks a child to spread it.
4. **Teachers who know Alfik.** The credited sources are what speak to them, and the 2026 grade 1 curriculum is a reason to look now. This is a step after the pilot verdict, not before.

Not now: app stores and paid ads. The stores' kids rules (a parental gate, no third-party analytics or ads) fit Jadwigo, but a store listing is a later choice. Jadwigo is a web app at jadwigo.pl for the hackathon.

## What this means for jadwigo

- The landing page (`dl-5ssh.4`) gets its copy from the lead the owner picks. Under the lead go "bezpłatnie", "bez reklam" and a credit line for Alfik and Khan Academy.
- Identity (`dl-w0b4.27`) and illustration (`dl-w0b4.18`) judge every option against the chosen lead. Under B (calm), a loud mascot fights the message.
- The pilot needs a Polish letter for parents, which no issue owns yet (proposed below).
- Site analytics stay cookieless (`dl-w0b4.14`). Any email form needs its own opt-in and a privacy page first (`dl-w0b4.10`).
