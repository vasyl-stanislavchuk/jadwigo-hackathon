# How Jadwigo differs

None of these apps is built around one child and their parent. Each is built around a shared course, a daily habit, a group quiz or a teacher's study set. The owner named these apps, and each claim comes from the app's own pages, its filings or the press.

:::table Each app, what it is built around, and how Jadwigo differs
| App | Built around | Personalises | Jadwigo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Khan Academy](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360030753412) | a shared course, worked through toward mastery, sometimes with a teacher's assignments | the pace through the same content | builds the content from this child's goals and last lesson |
| [Duolingo](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm) | a daily habit, held by streaks and points, and a free plan that meters practice with energy | not checked in these sources | no streaks, points or meter. The child stops when they say "enough for today" |
| [Kahoot](https://onlineteachinghub.education.purdue.edu/kahoot) | a quiz a whole group plays at once | nothing: one quiz for the group | one child, and many question types |
| [Quizlet](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-au/articles/39836814088845-Using-Assignments) | study sets, often assigned by a teacher to a class | the order of questions, in Learn mode | several goals set by a parent, mixed in one lesson |

## Details

- **Khan Academy.** Its [help centre](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360030753412) describes "self-paced mastery": students work through course content at their own pace, alone or with a teacher's assignments.
- **Kahoot.** The free plan offers multiple choice and true or false. Puzzles, polls and typed answers are in paid plans, according to a [Purdue teaching guide](https://onlineteachinghub.education.purdue.edu/kahoot).
- **Quizlet.** A teacher assigns a study mode from a set, such as Learn, Flashcards, Test or Match, and tracks the class, according to its [help centre](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-au/articles/39836814088845-Using-Assignments). Learn mode changes the order of questions, and the set is the same for every student.

## Duolingo's free plan

Duolingo gives its lessons away and charges for what surrounds them. "We intentionally do not put our learning content behind a paywall", says its [2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm). About 9% of monthly users paid at the end of 2025, according to the [same report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm).

- **Ads.** Free users see an ad at the end of each lesson, and subscribers don't ([2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm)).
- **Energy.** Since July 2025, energy has been replacing hearts. "Each exercise uses a unit of energy, regardless of whether the answer is right or wrong", and a run of right answers earns some back ([Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000165/q2fy25duolingo6-30x25share.htm)). Energy refills in about a day, or at once for an ad or gems ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/)).
- **Paying.** The app "pushes you to subscribe to its premium plan so that you can avoid the energy system entirely" ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/), 1 October 2025).
- **Results.** Duolingo wrote that energy "increased DAUs, median time spent learning well, and subscriber conversion" ([Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000165/q2fy25duolingo6-30x25share.htm)).
- **Streaks and trials.** About 43 million daily learners held a streak of 7 days or more at the end of 2025 ([2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm)). In 2026 Duolingo is offering longer free trials ([Q2 2026 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)).

Duolingo says energy, unlike hearts, does not penalise mistakes ([Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000165/q2fy25duolingo6-30x25share.htm)). Our own reading goes further, though Duolingo doesn't say it. A learner who makes many mistakes earns little energy back, so they reach the offer to pay sooner.

"Duolingo charges for mistakes" is out of date, and a parent who uses Duolingo could fairly dispute it. These wordings match the sources:
- "On Duolingo's free plan, every exercise uses energy, and paying removes the limit."
- "Duolingo is built around a daily streak, and its free plan meters how much you practise."

## Limits

- The owner chose these apps. The [go-to-market note](https://github.com/vasyl-stanislavchuk/jadwigo-hackathon/blob/main/docs/research/jadwigo-go-to-market.md) compared 8 more, and none of those puts the family first either. Neither is a market survey, so "no other app puts the family first" stays out of public copy.
- Some Duolingo learners may still see hearts, because energy began with "a small number of users" ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/)).
- Kahoot's split between free and paid question types comes from a university guide, not from Kahoot's pricing page.
