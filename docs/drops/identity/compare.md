# How Jadwigo differs

None of the products we compared is built around one child's goals with a parent beside them. Each one is built around something else: a shared course, a daily habit, a classroom game or a teacher's study set. On 27 October the owner named the products below. Each claim was then checked against the products' own pages or the press.

:::table Each product, what it is built around, and how Jadwigo differs
| Product | What it is built around | What it personalises | Jadwigo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Khan Academy](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360030753412) | a shared course, worked through toward mastery, sometimes with a teacher's assignments | the pace: each student moves through the same course content at their own speed | the content itself is built from this child's goals and from what they did last time |
| [Duolingo](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm) | a daily habit, held by streaks and points, and a free plan that meters practice with energy | not checked in these sources | no streaks or points, and no meter on practice. A child stops when they say "enough for today" |
| [Kahoot](https://onlineteachinghub.education.purdue.edu/kahoot) | a quiz game a whole group plays at once | one quiz for the whole group | one child, and many question types |
| [Quizlet](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-au/articles/39836814088845-Using-Assignments) | study sets, often assigned by a teacher to a class | the order of a set's questions, in Learn mode | many goals set by a parent, mixed in one sitting |

## Details

- **Khan Academy:** its [help centre](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360030753412) describes "self-paced mastery", in which students work through course content at their own pace, alone or with a teacher's assignments. Its pace is personal, and its course is shared.
- **Duolingo:** see the next section.
- **Kahoot:** the free plan offers multiple-choice and true-or-false questions. Puzzles, polls, typed answers and other types are in paid plans, according to a [Purdue teaching guide](https://onlineteachinghub.education.purdue.edu/kahoot).
- **Quizlet:** according to its [help centre](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-au/articles/39836814088845-Using-Assignments), a teacher assigns a study mode from a set, such as Learn, Flashcards, Test or Match. The teacher then tracks the class's progress. Learn mode adapts the order of questions, but the set is the same for every student.

## Duolingo and the free plan

Duolingo gives all of its learning content away and charges for everything around it. Its [2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm) calls this freemium: "We intentionally do not put our learning content behind a paywall." About 9% of monthly active users paid at the end of 2025, according to the same report.

**What a free learner meets.** Each step below is from Duolingo's own documents or the press.
- **Ads.** Free users see an ad at the end of each lesson, and paid subscribers don't ([2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm)).
- **Energy.** Since July 2025, energy has been replacing hearts. "Each exercise uses a unit of energy, regardless of whether the answer is right or wrong", and "learners get back energy when they get multiple answers correct in a row" ([Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000165/q2fy25duolingo6-30x25share.htm)). Energy "fully recharges in about a day", and a rewarded ad or gems buy a refill ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/)).
- **Paying.** The app "pushes you to subscribe to its premium plan so that you can avoid the energy system entirely" ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/), 1 October 2025).
- **Streaks.** At the end of 2025, about 43 million daily learners held a streak of 7 days or more ([2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm)).
- **Free trials.** In 2026 Duolingo is "offering longer free trials, which we expect to increase both revenue and DAUs" ([Q2 2026 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)).

**What energy did to conversion.** Duolingo wrote that energy "increased DAUs, median time spent learning well, and subscriber conversion", and that "we have rarely seen a feature move more than one of these metrics, let alone all three" ([Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000165/q2fy25duolingo6-30x25share.htm)).

**What energy means for mistakes.** Duolingo describes energy as a carrot, not a stick: "Unlike Hearts, which penalize mistakes, Energy is usage-based and rewards success" ([Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000165/q2fy25duolingo6-30x25share.htm)). A mistake no longer costs extra. Our reading of the same rules goes further. A learner who makes many mistakes earns little energy back and runs out sooner, so they meet the offer to pay sooner. Duolingo does not say this, so it is our inference, not their claim.

**How Jadwigo differs.** Jadwigo puts no meter on practice and no ad after a lesson, and keeps no streak. A child practises as long as they want, and stops when they say "enough for today".

**What we can say in public.** "Duolingo charges for mistakes" is out of date, and a parent who uses Duolingo today could fairly dispute it. These wordings match the sources:
- "On Duolingo's free plan, every exercise uses energy, and paying removes the limit."
- "Duolingo is built around a daily streak, and its free plan meters how much you practise."

## Positioning

Jadwigo is the one where the family is the unit. A parent sets the goals and sees the growth. A child sets the pace and can stop or ask for more. Each lesson is built for that one child.

## Limits of this comparison

- Products on this page were chosen by the owner. The [go-to-market note](https://github.com/vasyl-stanislavchuk/jadwigo-hackathon/blob/main/docs/research/jadwigo-go-to-market.md) compared 8 more, and none of those puts the family at the centre either. Neither note is a survey of the whole market.
- "No other product puts the family first" stays out of public copy until someone runs a wider search.
- Some Duolingo learners may still see hearts, because energy began with "a small number of users" ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/)). The section below describes energy, the system Duolingo is moving to.
- Kahoot's split between free and paid question types comes from a university guide, not from Kahoot's own pricing page.
