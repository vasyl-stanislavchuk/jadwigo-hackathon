# Strategy

Jadwigo grows by doing things that don't scale, in the sense of [Paul Graham's essay](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html). It serves one family well before it serves a class, and one class well before it serves a school.

## Not now

- **Paid ads** and **app stores**: the first families come through trust, and a store listing is a later choice.
- **jadwigo.kids and English**: jadwigo.pl in Polish comes first (`jadwigo-marketing/decisions/2026-10-27-jadwigo-launches-on-jadwigo-pl-only-for-now-jadwigo-kids-waits`).
- **Selling to schools**: the school ring waits for the pilot verdict.
- **Look, logo and illustrations**: they wait for the design system (`dl-w0b4.35`, `dl-w0b4.38`).

## Open

The charter still keeps technical parents in scope, who use Muse Code, the CLI or MCP, beside parents who want a service. The parents of one class are almost all the second kind. Whether technical parents wait until after the pilot is a question for the PRD (`dl-mol-mby`).
