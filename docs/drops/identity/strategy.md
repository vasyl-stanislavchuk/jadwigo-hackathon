# Strategy

Jadwigo grows by doing [things that don't scale](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html): one family first, then one class, then one school.

## Principles

:::table Principles and what each means in practice
| Principle | In practice |
| --- | --- |
| Our family is the first user | A change ships when it works at our own table, with our son. |
| Real goals decide what gets built | Nothing is built before a goal needs it. Our son's goals come first: multiplication and Polish spelling. |
| One ring at a time | The class is invited once the family ring works, and the school after the pilot verdict. |
| Trust, not ads | Families hear about Jadwigo from the class teacher, the parents' chat and a printed invitation. |
| Light to run | Servers store, check and serve. Cost follows use. |
:::

## Not now

- **Paid ads and app stores.** The first families come through people they know.
- **jadwigo.kids and English.** jadwigo.pl in Polish comes first.
- **Selling to schools.** The school ring waits for the pilot verdict.
- **Look, logo and illustrations.** They wait for the design system (`dl-w0b4.35`, `dl-w0b4.38`).

## Open

The charter still keeps technical parents in scope: parents who would use Muse Code, the CLI or MCP. Almost every parent in one class wants a service instead. Whether technical parents wait until after the pilot is a question for the PRD (`dl-mol-mby`).
