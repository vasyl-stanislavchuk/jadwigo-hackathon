# Voice

Each voice writes the same lines, so you can hear the difference.

Rules for every voice:
- The child is addressed as "ty", in the imperative.
- A miss is never a bare "źle". Words that `harsh.ts` bans in the playground, such as "błąd" or "porażka", never appear.
- No points, clocks or hurry. No line asks a child to get anything from a parent.
- No emoji, and no praise bigger than the answer.

## Voices

- **Spokojny przewodnik** (a calm guide): short sentences and few exclamation marks. This voice speaks the way a parent sits next to a child doing homework.
- **Życzliwa nauczycielka** (a kind teacher): warm and clear, the way a good teacher talks in the first school grades. This voice names what went well and shows the next step.
- **Ciekawy towarzysz** (a curious companion): playful, and plays with the "go" in the name ("Gotowi? Go!"). This voice treats a question as a riddle to solve together, never as a test.
