# Jadwigo: mission, value and story

Jadwigo is a learning app that a mum and a dad are building for their son. He practises multiplication and Polish spelling at home, and his parents chose those goals. In the owner's words: "The key audience for this project so far is me, my wife, and our kid."

What Jadwigo promises is on [Mission and value](promise.html). The other pages hold the story, the comparison with other apps, the strategy, and the copy in Polish.

## Already settled

- One child comes first. The hackathon entry is a by-product.
- A parent sets several goals for one child, and one lesson can mix subjects.
- Jadwigo grows from the family to the class, then the school.
- The family tells its own story, including what our son finds hard. Anyone else is named only after they agree.
- The marketing site may name the family. This drop says "our son".
- Only the school's patron, Queen Jadwiga, is named, never the school.
- Copy says what Jadwigo does. No slogans.

## Not yet tested

- No line has been tried on a parent, a child or a teacher.
- No native speaker has read the Polish (`dl-w0b4.40`).
- The comparison with other apps comes from their help pages, filings and the press, not from a market survey.
