# Story

A mum and a dad built Jadwigo for their son. He studies in a Polish school, and his parents set his first goals: basic multiplication, and Polish spelling, where he often makes mistakes. The family has given its consent to tell this story, including what he finds hard (`jadwigo-marketing/decisions/2026-10-27-the-family-shares-its-own-story-with-consent-and-others-are-name`).

## Why now

A lesson built from one child's own history used to take a private tutor. A family was never a big enough market for a product to be built around it. The owner said it on 27 October: "it always was not big enough. And now, with AI, it actually makes sense."

Jadwigo uses AI to write the next lesson from what the child did before. AI also judges an answer that the child wrote in their own words. Code checks every answer that has one exact form.

## O nas

This text is a draft for the "O nas" page on jadwigo.pl (`dl-w0b4.42`). On the site it can use the family's names (`dl-5ssh.41`). In this repo's docs it says "our son".

**Polski**

> Jesteśmy rodziną: mama, tata i nasz syn. Jadwigo zbudowaliśmy najpierw dla niego, żeby nauka w domu była czymś, co robimy razem.
>
> Zaczęliśmy od 2 celów: tabliczki mnożenia i ortografii, w której syn często się myli. Jedna lekcja może łączyć oba. Po każdej lekcji Jadwigo zapisuje, co poszło dobrze, gdzie syn miał trudność i w czym się mylił. Z tego powstaje następna lekcja.
>
> Syn może się pomylić, poprosić o więcej albo powiedzieć, że na dziś wystarczy. A my widzimy, jak rośnie.
>
> Teraz otwieramy Jadwigo dla jego klasy, potem dla całej szkoły. A może i dla Was?
>
> Jadwigo powstało podczas hackathonu Meta. Nazwę wzięło od patronki szkoły naszego syna, królowej Jadwigi.

**English**, for the hackathon pitch

> We are a family: a mum, a dad and our son. We built Jadwigo for him first, so that learning at home would be something we do together.
>
> We started with 2 goals: multiplication, and Polish spelling, where he often makes mistakes. One lesson can hold both. After each lesson, Jadwigo records what went well, where he struggled and which mistakes he made. The next lesson is built from that.
>
> He can make mistakes, ask for more, or say it's enough for today. And we see how he grows.
>
> Now we're opening Jadwigo to his class, then to his whole school. And maybe to you.
>
> Jadwigo started at the Meta hackathon. It is named after the patron of our son's school, Queen Jadwiga.

## Rings

Jadwigo grows one ring at a time, and each ring is reached through people the family already knows.

:::table Rings, in order
| Ring | Who | How they hear about Jadwigo | What has to be true first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family | mum, dad and our son | at home | our son plays, and his parents see him grow |
| Class | the parents of his class, 18 children ([pilot `dl-e5c4`](https://github.com/vasyl-stanislavchuk/jadwigo-hackathon/blob/main/docs/research/jadwigo-go-to-market.md)) | the class teacher, the parents' chat, a printed invitation | the family ring works |
| School | the other classes and their teachers | a poster, the teachers who already know Alfik | the pilot verdict |
| You | anyone who finds Jadwigo | word of mouth from parents who use it | the school ring works |
:::

The hackathon is where Jadwigo started, and it is not the goal. The [Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon) asks entrants to "Build for Your World", and Jadwigo is a parent building for their own child.
