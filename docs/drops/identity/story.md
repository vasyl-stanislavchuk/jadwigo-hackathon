# Story

A mum and a dad are building Jadwigo for their son, who goes to a Polish primary school. His first goals are multiplication and Polish spelling, where he often makes mistakes. The family agreed to tell this story, including what he finds hard.

## Why now

A lesson built from one child's history used to need a private tutor, and one family was too small a market to build for. In the owner's words: "it always was not big enough. And now, with AI, it actually makes sense."

AI writes the next lesson from what the child did before. It also judges an answer the child writes in their own words. Code checks every answer that has one exact form.

**English**, for the hackathon pitch

> We are a family: a mum, a dad and our son. We built Jadwigo for him first, so that we could learn with him at home.
>
> We started with multiplication and Polish spelling, where he often makes mistakes. After each lesson, Jadwigo records what went well, where he struggled and which mistakes he made. It builds the next lesson from that, sometimes from both subjects at once.
>
> He can make mistakes, ask for more, or say it's enough for today. We see what he can do now.
>
> Now we're inviting his class, then his whole school. And then maybe you.
>
> Jadwigo started at the Meta hackathon. It is named after the patron of our son's school, Queen Jadwiga.
