# Mission, vision and value

On 27 October the owner named the core promise: growth you can see, a next lesson built from the last one, and family bonding. A child learns without being rushed, has room to make mistakes, and can say "I want more" or "it's too much for today", "and all with parent awareness". Each option below puts that promise into words. Every Polish line is a draft for a native speaker.

## Mission

A mission says what Jadwigo is for, in one sentence. Each option leads with a different part of the promise.

:::table Mission options
| Option | English | Polski |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A, the family | Jadwigo helps a family learn together: the child at their own pace, the parent right beside them. | Jadwigo pomaga rodzinie uczyć się razem: dziecku we własnym tempie, a rodzicowi być tuż obok. |
| B, the child | Every child learns at their own pace, with room to make mistakes, and their parents see them grow. | Każde dziecko uczy się we własnym tempie, może się mylić, a rodzice widzą, jak rośnie. |
| C, the story | We built Jadwigo for our son, so that learning is something we share. | Zbudowaliśmy Jadwigo dla naszego syna, żeby nauka była czymś, co nas łączy. |
:::

## Vision

A vision says what the world looks like if Jadwigo works.

- **A, a tutor for every child:** every child has a tutor who knows them, and every parent knows what their child can do.
- **B, learning as family time:** learning at home becomes time a family shares, not a screen a child is left alone with.
- **C, one family at a time:** from one family to every family who wants it. First our son, then his class, his school, and maybe you.

## Value proposition

Jadwigo speaks to the parent and the child at once. The parent decides and the child studies, and both should feel at home.

:::table What Jadwigo promises, and to whom
| For | Promise | English | Polski |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Parent | Growth you can see | See what your child can do now that they couldn't a month ago. | Zobacz, co Twoje dziecko potrafi dziś, czego miesiąc temu jeszcze nie umiało. |
| Parent | Next lesson from the last | After each lesson, Jadwigo records what went well, where your child struggled and which mistakes they made. It builds the next lesson from that. | Po każdej lekcji Jadwigo zapisuje, co poszło dobrze, gdzie dziecko miało trudność i w czym się myliło. Z tego układa następną lekcję. |
| Parent | Your child's goals | Set the goals that matter to your child: several at once, from different subjects. One lesson can mix them. | Ustal cele, które są ważne dla Twojego dziecka: kilka naraz, z różnych przedmiotów. Jedna lekcja może je łączyć. |
| Child | Your pace, your say | You can make mistakes. You can say: I want more. Or: that's enough for today. | Możesz się pomylić. Możesz powiedzieć: chcę więcej. Albo: na dziś wystarczy. |
| Family | Together | Your child learns. You're right there, and you see it. | Dziecko się uczy. Ty jesteś obok i to widzisz. |
:::

Under every promise go the short lines that every competitor also says, so leaving them out would read as a gap:
- **Bezpłatnie.** Free.
- **Bez reklam.** No ads.
- **Zadania z Alfika Matematycznego i Khan Academy, zawsze ze źródłem.** Real tasks, each with its source.
