# Mission and value

## Promise

After each lesson, Jadwigo records what went well, where the child struggled and which mistakes they made. It builds the next lesson from that. A parent sets the goals, several at once and from different subjects, and sees what the child can do now. The child sets the pace: they can get things wrong, ask for more, or stop for today.

The owner put it this way: "giving them a space to say, 'I want more,' or, on the other hand, 'It's too much for today.' And all with parent awareness".

## Vision

- **A, a tutor for every child:** every child has a tutor who knows them, and every parent knows what their child can do.
- **B, learning as family time:** learning at home is time a family spends together, not a child alone with a screen.
- **C, one family at a time:** first our son, then his class, then his school, and then anyone who wants it.
