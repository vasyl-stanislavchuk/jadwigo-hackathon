This page is for a judge asking what Jadwigo remembers: each answer a child gives becomes
evidence, folded into what they can do, what is fading, and what is due again, ready for a parent
to read.

Today the product keeps the answers; it does not yet keep this folded picture per child. The fold
itself exists and is tested: `packages/core` turns one first try per question into a level
(attempted to mastered), a flag when a skill needs attention, and an FSRS card that says when the
skill is due again. A brief then sorts a goal's skills into reteach, review, new and recheck.
Code computes every one of those; no model ever recomputes a level.

<Diagram name="learner-knowledge" caption="Evidence in, a level and a due date out, then a brief." />

The vision is a small knowledge base per learner that a parent can open at any time: what the
child can do now in plain words, what needs attention, and what comes next, each line resting on
named attempts. [E1](/experiments/e1/) shows that fold moving on 12 invented learners over 3
weeks, including the awkward cases a real class has: the guesser, the one who is right only with
help, and the one who only practises at weekends.

2 rules the vision keeps from today's decisions:

- A parent never reads a level name, a score or a percentage. The page says in words what is
  going well and what needs attention.
- Nothing is planned blindly. The brief proposes; a parent accepts or changes it.
