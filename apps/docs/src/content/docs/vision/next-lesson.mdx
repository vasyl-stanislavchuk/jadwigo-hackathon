This page is for a judge pressing the button that is not there yet: from a child's mastery and due
dates, Jadwigo draws the next lesson from the bank and can say why each question is in it.

The parts exist separately today. The brief sorts a learner's skills into reteach, review, new
and recheck. The bank search finds question sets, and `jw draw` picks questions a learner has not
seen, or ones FSRS says are due, holding back anything seen too recently. Every question drawn or
left out carries its reason, and the draw rules per lesson kind are written down as provisional:
the owner settles them, and each draw says `rule_settled: false` until then.

<Diagram name="next-lesson" caption="From a brief to a lesson, with a reason for every question." />

The vision is the button between them: a parent asks for the next lesson, and Jadwigo builds it
from this learner's brief, not from a course order. [E2](/experiments/e2/) runs that join on the
12 invented learners of E1, against the real bank, and shows where it gives a full lesson and
where the bank has nothing to give yet.
