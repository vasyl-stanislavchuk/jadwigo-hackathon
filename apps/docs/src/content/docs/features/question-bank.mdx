Lessons are built from a bank of borrowed, credited questions, and only a question the bank has
checked reaches a child.

## How it works

### What's in it

The bank holds Khan Academy maths items for grades 1 to 4 and Alfik Matematyczny tasks, in Polish
and English. It has its own search, by topic, grade, language and question type, so a lesson can
ask for the kinds of question a child needs.

### Lesson-ready

A lesson takes only a lesson-ready question. Ready means the bank checked that the question
compiles, that its key grades its own answer, that every picture loads, and that the page draws
it without an error. A question the bank hasn't checked waits. A question a person flagged is out.

### Credits

Every question carries its source, a reference, its licence and its original language. A Khan
Academy item shows Khan's credit and its CC BY-NC-SA licence, with a line that says what changed.
An Alfik task names Alfik Matematyczny. The child sees the credit beside the question, and the
card it opens names the licence.

## Limits

- **No person has reviewed the enrichment yet.** The topics and summaries that models wrote for
  search are all marked unreviewed ([E4](/experiments/e4/)).
- **The search test flags the current index.** It finds more than the last one, but declines
  fewer of the requests it should decline ([E5](/experiments/e5/)).
