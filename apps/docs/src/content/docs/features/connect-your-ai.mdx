A parent who already talks to an AI app can connect it to Jadwigo and make lessons from that chat.

<Diagram name="connect-your-ai" />

## How it works

### Signing in

Jadwigo serves a remote MCP server at `https://app.jadwigo.pl/api/mcp`. The parent adds that URL
to their AI app, and the app signs in by OAuth 2.1. The parent signs in to Jadwigo and approves
the app on a consent page. The app's token then carries the same rights as the parent's own.

### Making a lesson

Through the server's tools, the parent's AI reads only their own family's data, plus the question
bank. It writes the lesson's words. Jadwigo's own server checks and keeps the lesson. When the
parent says yes in their AI chat, Jadwigo publishes the checked lesson for 1 named child and
answers with that child's [play link](/features/share-and-play/).

### Whose data goes where

A child's data, names included, may go to the AI app the parent connected. That's the parent's
own export of their family's data, and the consent page says so.

## Limits

- **Nobody has run it end to end yet.** On 2026-11-04 the server and its sign-in answered on prod,
  but no parent's AI app had connected and made a lesson. Treat the table below as the shape of
  the connection, not a tested walkthrough.
- **A disconnected app keeps working for up to 1 hour,** until its current token expires.

## Every client
