This page is for a parent who already talks to an AI app and wants that app to make lessons in
Jadwigo. It folds in `dl-1u6fn.12`.

Jadwigo serves a remote MCP at `https://app.jadwigo.pl/api/mcp`. It is stateless Streamable HTTP
with JSON replies and no server-sent events. The parent's AI app connects by OAuth 2.1, with
Supabase Auth as the authorization server. A call with no token gets a `401` whose
`WWW-Authenticate` header names the protected-resource metadata. The metadata names the
authorization server. The parent signs in and approves on a consent page at `/oauth/consent`.
The AI app's token then carries the same rights as the parent's own web token. A revoked app
keeps its current token until it expires, at most 1 hour.

Through the tools, the parent's AI reads only their own family's pod, plus the question bank. It
writes the lesson's words. Jadwigo's server-side verbs draft, check, and keep the lesson. The
parent's yes in their own AI chat is the confirmation: `lesson_publish` publishes the checked
lesson for 1 named child and answers that child's play link. A child's data, names included, may
go to the AI app the parent connected. It is the parent's own export of their family's data.
