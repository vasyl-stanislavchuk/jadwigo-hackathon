This page is for a judge who wants to see how Jadwigo credits the questions it borrows.

Every bank question carries a credit: its source, a reference, its licence, and its original
language. A Khan Academy item shows Khan's credit and its CC BY-NC-SA licence, with a line that
says what changed. An Alfik task names Alfik Matematyczny. The credit sits beside the question's
instruction, and the card it opens names the licence. Parents never see Khan's internal level
names.

On prod the credit does not draw. `dl-1eieq.3` found nothing on `main` provided the lesson kit's
credit context, so the credit row drew nothing in `/play`. Parts of the fix have landed: a
question the AI draws from the bank now keeps its credit, and the page wire is getting a credit
field of its own. The rest is in progress, so this page is labelled `main`, not `live`.
