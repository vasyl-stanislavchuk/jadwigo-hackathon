This experiment is for a judge pricing the happy path: 3 homework worksheets we drew ourselves go
from photo to lesson through the local runner, with the time, the model cost and the checks
recorded.

<ExperimentResult id="e6" />

Each worksheet is an invented grade 2 or 3 homework page, drawn as HTML and rendered to a photo:
multiplication, Polish spelling, and a shopping word problem. A parent message with the photo is
accepted exactly as the API accepts it, then drained by the runner's own engine on the local
database with the product model. The one stand-in is the photo reader: on prod the bytes come
from the uploads bucket, here from the worksheet file; the JPEG the model reads is the same
either way. Cost and tokens come from each run's own model records, the rows the ledger sums
per run.

![The invented grade 2 multiplication worksheet: 8 sums, 4 already answered, 1 of them
wrong](../../../assets/experiments/e6/w1-mnozenie.png)

The multiplication and word-problem worksheets became lessons. The agent searched the bank,
drafted, checked, and presented a card; each lesson was then played once by grading every step
with the player's own judge and the key's solution. The multiplication lesson's check reported
18 contract findings and a skill id that is not a skill id, and the card was still presented:
the check informs the parent, it does not gate the lesson. That draft also fails to compile
(its help words have the wrong shape), so its play graded the file's own steps directly, not a
compiled lesson. The spelling worksheet became no
lesson at all: the bank holds no Polish spelling questions, the follow-up call threw on a
missing bank route, and the turn ended with no error for the parent. That failure is filed as
issue `dl-r2pla`.

## Every worksheet in the run

| Worksheet | Grade | Wall time | Model calls | Cost (USD) | What came out |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Multiplication | 2 | 44.2 s | 9 | 0.0024 | A 6-step draft; its steps played 6 of 6 correct (draft itself fails compile) |
| Polish spelling: rz/ż, ó/u | 3 | 5.7 s | 2 | 0.0001 | No lesson: no bank questions, then a dead-lettered step |
| Word problems: shopping and money | 3 | 38.3 s | 8 | 0.0023 | A 6-step lesson, played 6 of 6 correct |

![The invented grade 3 spelling worksheet: rz/ż and ó/u gaps, some already

![The invented grade 3 word-problem worksheet: shopping, change from
50 zł](../../../assets/experiments/e6/w3-zadanie.png)

Per family, one lesson from a photo costs about a quarter of a cent and under a minute of wall
time: 162,303 input tokens across the 3 runs, 134,386 of them cached, and 8,808 output tokens.
That is the number per-family pricing and credits can be seeded
from, and it says the light-to-run value is real. The full per-run steps, findings and plays
are under `detail` in this experiment's JSON.
