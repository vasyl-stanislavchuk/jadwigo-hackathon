This run tests whether the next lesson can be drawn from what code knows about a child, with a
reason for every question in or out. It's the second step of
[the learning loop](/vision/learning-loop/).

<ExperimentResult id="e2" />

## Question

Given a learner's brief from [E1](/experiments/e1/), can a draw from the real question bank make a
lesson that fits that learner?

## Setup

Each of the 12 briefs from E1 becomes a list of needs, and the lesson takes the first 3 needs that
draw questions. Bank sets are found by skill in a local copy of the real bank, 412 set pages in
all. The draw rules are provisional: unseen questions for practice, a due question or its twin for
review, and spacing kept. Then 3 model judges rated each lesson's fit to its learner from 1 to 5,
with reasons. The learners are the team's inventions, and the judges are models, not teachers.

## Result

11 of 12 learners got a lesson of 15 to 18 questions, 183 questions in all, each with its reason.

## What failed

- **The fit is poor: 2.39 of 5.** The judges gave the same reasons again and again. The draw
  reteaches a skill the learner already does well while a weaker one is ignored, and 18 questions
  is long for one sitting.
- **1 learner got no lesson.** That learner's brief is 4 Polish spelling skills, and no bank set
  carries those skills yet. The reasons the draw recorded say exactly that.

## Cost

$0.09 for the 36 judge calls. The draw itself ran locally, for $0.
