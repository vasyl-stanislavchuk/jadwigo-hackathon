[What's built](/built/) is one lesson from one photo. What's next is everything after that lesson:
Jadwigo keeps teaching the same child, draws from more subjects, and reads answers a child writes
in their own words.

The areas in the table aren't in the product yet. Each one was tried first in an experiment on Meta's
Muse models, with invented learners and invented answers only. The table says what each run
showed, including what didn't work yet.

## Learning loop

Nothing acts on a child's answers on its own yet. [The learner knowledge
base](/built/#learner-knowledge) is built: every answer becomes a level, a flag and a due date per
skill, and the parent sees it. The loop is what's missing: drawing the next lesson from that
picture, and writing the parent a report each week, with no one pressing a button.

The part that has to be right is the fold, and that part is tested hardest:

- **Code computes every level.** A model only writes words, from numbers code counted, and code
  checks those words before they're saved. A model can't invent a level.
- **Golden cases hold the fold.** 21 planted cases each pin one rule of the levels. A right answer
  only with help doesn't count as known. Mastered needs 2 more days and a mixed lesson. A miss
  drops a skill at most one level.
- **It separates real patterns.** [E1](/experiments/e1/) folded 1,301 first tries from 12 invented
  learners over 21 days, for $0. The guesser, the child who is right only with help and the
  weekend-only child each came out clearly different.

The 2 steps after the fold aren't there yet. Lessons drawn from it fit at 2.39 of 5
([E2](/experiments/e2/)), and 1 of 12 reports passed the code check ([E3](/experiments/e3/)). Both
fail on the choice and the shape, not on the facts, and both are the next work.

## Every area

| Area | What it would do | What the experiments showed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Learning loop](/vision/learning-loop/) | Turn each answer into what the child knows, draw the next lesson from it, and write the parent a weekly report. | The fold works on 12 invented learners ([E1](/experiments/e1/)). | [Growing the bank](/vision/growing-the-bank/) | Read new question sets for more subjects and grades, enrich and index them with Meta models, and test search before a child sees them. | Enriching the first 143 sets and their 2,738 questions cost $3.79 ([E4](/experiments/e4/)). | [Judging answers](/vision/judging-answers/) | Mark a child's own-words answer against criteria set before the task, kindly. | 2 models agreed with the team's labels on about 95% of 80 invented answers ([E7](/experiments/e7/)). |
