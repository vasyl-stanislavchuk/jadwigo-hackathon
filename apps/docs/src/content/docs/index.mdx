Jadwigo is a learning app for children in the first school grades, in Polish and English. The
parent's job takes 1 photo. Everything after it is the app's job.

The happy path has 4 steps. A parent signs in and adds a child. The parent photographs a homework
page in the chat. Jadwigo reads the photo, draws questions from the question bank, and builds a
lesson of at most 20 questions. The parent gives the lesson, prints it, or sends its link. The
child opens the link and plays, with no sign-in.

Every feature page carries 1 of 3 labels. `live` means it runs on prod today. `main` means the
code is on `main` and not yet on prod. `vision` means it is not built, and an experiment page
shows the idea working on invented learners. Labels were checked on 2026-11-03.
