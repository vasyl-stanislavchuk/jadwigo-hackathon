The parent's job is 1 photo. A parent signs in and sends a photo of a homework page in the chat.
Jadwigo reads it, draws up to 20 questions from the question bank, and checks the lesson. The
parent sends the child a link, and the child plays with no sign-in.

Every feature and vision page carries a label: `live` (on app.jadwigo.pl today), `main` (merged,
not yet deployed) or `vision` (tried in an experiment on invented learners, not built). Each
section opens with a one-page overview, so you can stop there or go deeper.
