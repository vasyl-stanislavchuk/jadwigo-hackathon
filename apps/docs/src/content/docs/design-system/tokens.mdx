## Type, shape, space and motion

The same in light and dark. On a phone, under 600 px wide, the type steps take smaller values:
the prompt stays at 28 px or more and an option at 24 px or more, and a task's readout
(`--jw-size-target`) goes from 44 px to 36 px. Under reduced motion every duration is 0.

The `--jw-page-*` steps are the adult's scale on an app page, the parent's screens: a first
heading of 28 px (26 on a phone), a second of 22 px (20 on a phone), and body text, a third
heading and a label at 17 px with a line height of 1.5. A lesson keeps the child's scale.
