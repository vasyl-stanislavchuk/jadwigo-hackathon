A token is a named value, a colour, a size or a duration, that every part of the app reads in
place of a literal. This page is for anyone styling a part or checking what a token comes to: it
lists every one, with its value in light and dark.

The [Design system](/design-system/#3-tiers-of-tokens) overview explains the 3 tiers they come in.

## Sizes on a phone and on a parent's page

The type, shape, space and motion steps are the same in light and dark. On a phone, under 600 px
wide, the type steps take smaller values: the prompt stays at 28 px or more and an option at
24 px or more, and a task's readout (`--jw-size-target`) goes from 44 px to 36 px. Under reduced
motion every duration is 0.

The `--jw-page-*` steps are the adult's scale on an app page, the parent's screens: a first
heading of 28 px (26 on a phone), a second of 22 px (20 on a phone), and body text, a third
heading and a label at 17 px with a line height of 1.5. A lesson keeps the child's scale.
