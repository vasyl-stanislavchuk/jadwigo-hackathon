A token is a named value, a colour, a size or a duration, that every part of the app reads in
place of a literal. This page is for anyone styling a part or checking what a token comes to: it
lists every token in every stylesheet the app loads, with its value in light and dark.

The [Design system](/design-system/#3-tiers-of-tokens) overview explains the 3 tiers Zeszyt's
colours come in.

## Where the tokens are set

The app loads one stylesheet, which brings in the others. Most of what a page shows is Zeszyt's
(`foundation.css`): its roles, aliases and primitives, and its type, shape, space and motion
steps. The rest are Tailwind's type steps and a few layout sizes (`tokens.css`), the lesson's
sizes (`kids.css` and `stage.css`), 3 palettes of their own (`tokens.css`), and Tailwind's names
for all of them (`tailwind.css`). A few properties a rule sets only for its own part, such as the
edge a picked option stands on, are not tokens and are not listed.

## Sizes on a phone and on a parent's page

The type, shape, space and motion steps are the same in light and dark. On a phone, under 600 px
wide, the type steps take smaller values: the prompt stays at 28 px or more and an option at
24 px or more, and a task's readout (`--jw-size-target`) goes from 44 px to 36 px. Under reduced
motion every duration is 0.

The `--jw-page-*` steps are the adult's scale on an app page, the parent's screens: a first
heading of 28 px (26 on a phone), a second of 22 px (20 on a phone), and body text, a third
heading and a label at 17 px with a line height of 1.5. A lesson keeps the child's scale.

### The parent's density

Inside the signed-in parent app the sizes are denser, because a parent works there at a desk
(decision `dl-xo0q`). `DensityScope value="parent"` sets `data-density="parent"`, and a dialog,
sheet or popover opened inside it carries the attribute through its portal. There, body text is
14 px and a small line 12.5 px (`--jw-page-small`), a section title 20 px, a control 34 px high,
corners 10 px and 8 px, lines 1 px (`--jw-border`), and the action stands on a 3 px edge. Sign-in
and public pages keep the sizes above. A lesson shown inside the parent app, such as a step's
preview, gets every child size back.

The app frame has its own sizes, each a utility (`w-jw-shell-side`, `h-jw-shell-bar`):

- the sidebar, 232 px, folded to a 60 px rail;
- the 56 px line the sidebar's logo row and the top bar share;
- the chat column, 360 px, and the details column, 290 px;
- the 52 px strip a folded panel leaves;
- the 68 px band under the chat and the lesson;
- the first screen's 720 px box;
- a dialog, 440 px wide with 14 px corners.

`border-jw` (and `border-t-jw` and the other sides) draws a line at `--jw-border`, so it follows
the density.

Tailwind's own type steps (`text-xl` and the rest) are set outside Tailwind's theme layer, so they
win over its defaults: `text-xl` is 20 px here.

## The lesson's sizes

2 stylesheets size the lesson, and they apply in different places.

- **The minimum sizes** (`kids.css`) are set at the root, so they hold everywhere: a tap target
  anywhere in a lesson is at least `--kid-tap-min`. A block may make its own controls bigger,
  never smaller.
- **The lesson frame's sizes** (`stage.css`) are set on the frame around a step
  (`.kid-step-shell`), again for each frame shape it picks by input and screen (`data-frame`:
  phone, tablet, tablet-sideways, phone-sideways, laptop), or on one part. Outside a step they are
  unset. The top bar's height is the exception: it is set at the root, since the page scrolls
  around the bar. The docked tools' height, `--kid-tray-height`, is measured and set by the
  frame's code, not by a stylesheet.

## The special palettes

3 palettes sit outside Zeszyt's roles, each for one kind of content, and each colour holds 4.5:1
on its ground.

- **Maths text**: the 10 colours a maths prompt can paint a number or a word in. Khan's colour
  macros (`\blueD`, `\greenD` and the rest) fold into these. Each has a light tone for the white
  paper and a dark one for the night page.
- **Money**: drawn coins and notes, never pictures of real money. Grosze coins are copper, złoty
  coins gold, notes a pale green paper, each in a day and a night tone. The sizes go by value:
  small grosze, larger grosze, złoty coins, notes.
- **The terminal card**, where a command is shown to copy, is **always dark**, in both themes, so
  its colours have one value each. They are the palette from before Zeszyt, kept on purpose until
  a lesson shows a terminal. A page reads them directly: they have no Tailwind utility, since a
  utility has a light and a dark value.

## Tailwind's names

A part writes a Tailwind utility, never a `var()` in a class, and each utility reads one token. A
colour makes every colour utility (`bg-jw-act`, `text-jw-act`, `border-jw-act`); a size makes
every sizing and spacing one (`min-h-kid-tap`, `size-kid-key`, `gap-kid-choice`). Each resolves
where it is used, so it follows the theme wherever `[theme='dark']` is set.
