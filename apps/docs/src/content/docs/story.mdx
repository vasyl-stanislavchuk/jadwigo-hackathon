This page is for a judge who wants to know why Jadwigo looks the way it does. Every product call
was recorded as a dated decision before the code was written. This is that record, in order. Each
decision is cited by slug in the table at the end. Names of family members never appear.

<Diagram name="story-timeline" />

## 2026-10-24: one repo, one model family

The 6 founding decisions of this day predate the `jadwigo-hackathon` record, so they are told
here without slugs. Every later decision is cited in the table at the end.

Jadwigo got 1 new monorepo for the hackathon build.
The service is TypeScript on Lambda.
Every AI workflow runs on Meta's API.
The question bank is never committed to the repo,
and it embeds with OpenAI `text-embedding-3-large`.
Agents ship fast: squash-merge after CI and a light automatic review.

## 2026-10-26 to 10-27: 1 board, 6 workstreams

The build runs as 1 meta project and 6 workstream projects on 1 shared board.
Each workstream builds where its code lives.
Voice stayed out of scope until the core worked.
A parent may set many goals for 1 child, and 1 sitting may mix subjects.

## 2026-10-29 to 10-31: lessons, curriculum, the dream

Parents never see Khan's level names, and Khan questions keep their credits.
The first `lesson_draft` read item bodies from the local bank and played on jadwigo-dev.
The curriculum is shared across pods, and skills live in each pod.
The dream, a report over a learner's results, runs on demand: code computes the digest and AI
writes only the narrative.
A pod's edited copy of a shared skill is never offered back to the shared catalogue.

## 2026-11-01: the chat, and learner data by design

The parent's chat lives in a side panel and a full screen.
A learner's data, names included, may reach any Meta model for the parent's own request about
their own child.
Lessons may be English, with the language taken from the goal.
A break after a merge is fixed forward at once, never reverted.

## 2026-11-02: chat-first, and the parent's own AI

The parent app became chat-first: account in the top bar, lesson on demand, no footer.
A play link with a learner in it starts as that child with no sign-in.
A parent's own AI app connects by OAuth 2.1, with Supabase Auth as the authorization server.
Through it, the parent's AI writes the lesson and Jadwigo's verbs draft, check, and keep it.
The parent's yes in their own AI chat is the confirmation for `lesson_publish`.
A lesson holds at most 20 questions, however it was made.
PostHog runs after consent, with session replay and inputs masked.

## 2026-11-03: the demo cut

Until the 4 November demo the goal is the parent happy path: photo, lesson, share, the child
plays. Learner knowledge waits as product work.
Printing joined the demo scope as a server-side PDF,
rendered natively from paper-first HTML and CSS with WeasyPrint.
The lesson opens in a preview modal from the chat card, not in a column.
These docs show the vision beside what is live, each part labelled.

## 2026-11-04: demo day

No decision is recorded for demo day yet. The site you are reading is the record so far.
