A parent can ask at any time for their Jadwigo account and the data linked to it to be deleted. This
includes an account they sign in to with Google or Facebook.

## How to ask

1. Write an email to **[CONTACT_EMAIL_TBD]**.
2. Send it from the address you sign in to Jadwigo with. That is how we find your account.
3. Put "Data deletion" in the subject. You don't need to explain anything.

## What happens next

1. We confirm that we have your request. If you write from an address other than the one on the
   account, we ask you to confirm from the account's address.
2. We delete the account and the data linked to it: in Supabase, the email address and the link to
   your Google or Facebook account; in Jadwigo, your children's progress in the lessons.
3. We write to you once the data is deleted, no later than one month after we receive your request.

A deleted account can't be restored. You can set up a new one later.

## Disconnecting Jadwigo from Google or Facebook

You can also remove Jadwigo's access to your account:

- Google: in your [Google account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/connections), in the list of
  apps connected to your account.
- Facebook: under Settings and privacy, Settings, Apps and websites.

Disconnecting alone doesn't delete the data Jadwigo already has. To delete it, write to us as above.
