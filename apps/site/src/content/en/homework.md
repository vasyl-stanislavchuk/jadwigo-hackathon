Take a photo of the problem your child got stuck on. Jadwigo sees what was hard and builds a short lesson from it.

1. You take a photo of the homework.
2. Jadwigo figures out what the problems were about and where your child got stuck.
3. Your child plays a short lesson, and you see how it went.

## How to start

[Sign in](/en/sign-in/) and send the photo in your chat with Jadwigo AI. Without an account, you can play one of the [lessons to start with](/en/#lessons).
