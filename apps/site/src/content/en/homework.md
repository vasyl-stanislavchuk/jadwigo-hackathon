Soon you'll take a photo of the problem your child got stuck on. Jadwigo will see what was hard and build a short lesson from it.

1. You take a photo of the homework.
2. Jadwigo figures out what the problems were about and where your child got stuck.
3. Your child plays a short lesson, and you see how it went.

## For now

Play one of the [lessons to start with](/en/#lessons), no sign-in needed. Parents who [sign in](/en/sign-in/) will be the first to try homework photos.
