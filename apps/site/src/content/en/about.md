I'm a dad of 2 crazy boys. We recently moved to Poland: new language, no grandparents, few friends. David's Polish is already better than ours.

His teacher gives the whole class one homework, and each child gets stuck somewhere different. David's hard parts are multiplication and Polish spelling. We use Khan sometimes. It gives a child their own pace, but we needed their own content: the thing that came home from school today.

So I built Jadwigo for us. We're its first users. A photo of his homework becomes a lesson made for him, with a link he opens or a sheet he does on paper. We see what he can already do.

**Where the name comes from.** It's a play on words: Jadwiga, the queen, and "go", moving. Moving forward and growing under her warm wings. Queen Jadwiga is a school's patron. She was crowned "king" in 1384, and her jewels helped restore the Kraków Academy. In our pictures she's a little queen, and her pencil-case court stands beside her: Gumka the eraser, Ołówek the pencil and Linijka the ruler.

**What's next.** First David's class, then his school. And then maybe you, if you want to be close to your child's learning.
