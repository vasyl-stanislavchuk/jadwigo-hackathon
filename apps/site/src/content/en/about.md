We are a family: a mum, a dad and our son. We built Jadwigo for him first, so we could learn together with him at home.

<figure class="frame"><figcaption>A photo of our family goes here</figcaption></figure>

We started with the times tables and the Polish spelling our son often gets wrong. After each lesson Jadwigo notes what went well, where he found it hard and which mistakes he made. The next lesson is built from that.

Our son can get things wrong, ask for more or say that's enough for today. We see what he can already do.

<figure class="frame"><figcaption>A photo of our son learning goes here</figcaption></figure>

## Where the name comes from

Jadwigo is named after Queen Jadwiga, the patron of our son's school. "Jadwigo!" is how you call out her name in Polish. In the lessons Jadwiga is the queen of the exercise book, and her class stands beside her: Ola, Tymek, Zoja and Marko.

## What's next

Now we are inviting our son's class, then the whole school. And then maybe you.
